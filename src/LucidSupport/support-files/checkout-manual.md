# Checkout Help Manual

This manual covers everything customers need to know about the checkout process, including payment, shipping, returns, and account setup.

## Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods:

- **Credit/Debit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. Enter your 16-digit card number, expiration date (MM/YY), and the 3-digit CVV on the back of your card.
- **PayPal**: Click "Pay with PayPal" to be redirected to PayPal's secure login. You can pay from your PayPal balance or a linked bank account.
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay**: Available on supported devices. Look for the digital wallet option at checkout.
- **Gift Cards**: Enter your gift card number and PIN in the designated fields. Gift cards can be combined with other payment methods.

All payment information is encrypted using TLS 1.3 and is never stored on our servers. We are PCI DSS Level 1 certified.

If your card is declined, check that: the card number is correct, the expiration date hasn't passed, you have sufficient funds, and your billing address matches what's on file with your bank.

## Shipping Options

We offer several shipping tiers:

- **Standard Shipping** (5-7 business days): Free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99.
- **Express Shipping** (2-3 business days): $12.99 for all orders.
- **Next Day Delivery** (1 business day): $24.99, order by 2 PM local time for same-day processing.
- **International Shipping**: Available to over 40 countries. Rates calculated at checkout based on weight and destination. Customs and duties are the responsibility of the recipient.

Tracking information is emailed within 24 hours of shipment. You can also view tracking status in your account under "My Orders."

If your package is marked as delivered but you haven't received it, check with neighbors, your building's front desk, or contact us within 7 days.

## Return Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase.

- **Standard Returns**: Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in original packaging, and with all tags attached.
- **Defective Items**: If you received a defective or damaged item, contact us within 14 days. We'll send a prepaid return label and issue a full refund or replacement.
- **Digital Products**: Digital downloads and gift cards are non-refundable once activated.
- **Sale Items**: Items purchased at 50% off or more are final sale and cannot be returned.

To initiate a return, go to "My Orders," find the order, and click "Return Item." You'll receive a prepaid shipping label via email.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. The refund will be applied to your original payment method.

## Account Setup

Creating an account is quick and gives you access to order tracking, saved addresses, and faster checkout.

To create an account:
1. Click "Sign Up" in the top navigation bar.
2. Enter your email address and create a password (minimum 8 characters, must include at least one number and one letter).
3. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox.
4. Optionally, add a shipping address and payment method for faster checkout.

If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page. A reset link will be sent to your registered email address.

For security, we recommend: using a unique password not shared with other sites, enabling two-factor authentication in account settings, and reviewing your account activity regularly.

## Troubleshooting

### Card Declined
If your credit card is declined during checkout, try the following:
1. Verify the card number, expiration date, and CVV are entered correctly.
2. Ensure your billing address matches the address on file with your bank.
3. Check that you have sufficient funds or credit available.
4. Try a different card or payment method.
5. Contact your bank - they may have flagged the transaction for security.

### Coupon Code Not Working
If a promotional code isn't applying:
- Check that the code hasn't expired (expiration dates are listed in the promotion email).
- Verify the code applies to items in your cart (some codes exclude certain categories or brands).
- Ensure you've met the minimum order amount if required.
- Only one promotional code can be used per order.

### Page Not Loading
If the checkout page isn't loading properly:
- Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again.
- Try a different browser (we recommend Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge).
- Disable browser extensions that might interfere (ad blockers, privacy tools).
- If on mobile, try switching between WiFi and cellular data.
- Contact our support team if the issue persists.

### Order Confirmation Missing
If you haven't received an order confirmation email:
- Check your spam/junk folder.
- Verify the email address on your account is correct.
- Allow up to 15 minutes for the confirmation to arrive.
- Log into your account and check "My Orders" to verify the order was placed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change my order after placing it?**
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed. Go to "My Orders" and click "Modify" if within the window.

**Do you offer price matching?**
We offer a 14-day price match guarantee. If an item you purchased drops in price within 14 days, contact us for a refund of the difference.

**How do I use multiple gift cards?**
You can add up to 5 gift cards per order. Enter each card in the gift card field one at a time. Any remaining balance stays on the card for future use.

**Is my payment information safe?**
Yes. We use industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.3) for all transactions. We are PCI DSS Level 1 certified and never store your full credit card number on our servers. Card information is tokenized through our payment processor.

**Can I ship to multiple addresses?**
Currently, each order ships to a single address. For multiple shipping addresses, please place separate orders.
